Add the sizes customers can pick, then edit the measurement table. You can add extra columns (Chest, Rise, etc.) and extra rows.

First column is the size label. Other columns are measurements. Leave a cell blank if that size does not need that measurement.

Easy photo guide

  1. Click Choose files and pick 2–5 clear product photos.
  2. Click Upload images and wait until it says success.
  3. Photo links will auto-fill in “paste the URL”.
  4. First photo is the main shop image — put your best one first.
  5. Edit sizes and the size chart in step 2.
